## How Forever's PvP works

Forever replaced the original standing-based Honor System. Progression has two separate parts ([Blizzard: How PvP Progression in WoW Forever Works](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24303316/how-pvp-progression-in-world-of-warcraft-forever-works)):

- **Honor** is a currency (Honor Points) earned by defeating enemy players, completing battlegrounds and PvP quests, with more in the highlighted holiday-week battleground. It buys PvP gear: a blue set, and an epic set made by upgrading a blue piece with an Emboldened Seal, whose purchase requires a rank. Honor does not raise rank.
- **Rank Points** come from battlegrounds and PvP quests that grant them (holiday-week battlegrounds give extra), and from the season's weekly PvP tasks. World PvP kills give Honor, not Rank Points, so killing players in the open world alone does not leave Civilian. Rank 0 is Civilian; ranks go up to 14 (High Warlord / Grand Marshal).
- Ranks have a **weekly cap** that rises each week of the season up to the season's maximum. Progress cannot pass the current week’s cap; whether points earned at the cap carry over is not documented.
- Rewards unlock by rank and are bought with Honor from the Hall of Legends in Orgrimmar (Horde) or the Champion's Hall in Stormwind (Alliance): rank 1 tabard, 2 PvP trinket, 3 cloaks, 4 necklace, 5 combat potions, 6 tabard, 7 battle standard, 8 epic bracers/belt token, 9 epic boots token, 10 epic gloves token, 11 mounts, 12 epic legs/shoulders token, 13 epic chest/helm token, 14 epic weapons.
- The character pane (C) → Player vs. Player tab shows rank, Rank Points and rewards.

## Reading the pvp section

- rank: current rank (0 = Civilian) and title as the game shows it; points of nextRankPoints toward the next rank; seasonPoints is the season total; weeklyCapRank and weeklyCapPoints are this week's cap, weeklyCapIncrease how much it rose this week; maxRank and seasonMaxPoints are the season's end cap; nextReward is the next rank with rewards and their descriptions.
- honor: Honor Points amount and max, plus earnedThisWeek when the game reports it.
- kills: honorable and dishonorable kills today and yesterday, and lifetime honorable kills.
- season: the PvP season number and seconds until it ends, when known.

When seasonPoints reaches weeklyCapPoints, say the character is at this week’s cap and the rank can advance again when the cap rises. For a rank goal, point to battlegrounds and Rank Point quests, and use the reading for how far the next rank is. Do not invent honor per kill or Rank Points per battleground; say they are not known when asked.
